# Aptitude & Pseudocode Preparation Resources

A collection of handouts and practice materials for students preparing
for campus placements and recruitment assessments.

This repository brings together resources for **quantitative aptitude,
logical reasoning, and pseudocode/programming logic** in one place.

## How to Use These Resources

1.  Choose a topic from the repository.
2.  Read the corresponding handout and understand the concepts.
3.  Work through the examples and practice questions in the material.
4.  Revisit topics that need more practice.
5.  For pseudocode, focus on tracing the logic step by step rather than
    memorising answers.

## Who Is This For?

-   Students preparing for campus placements.
-   Freshers preparing for aptitude and reasoning rounds.
-   Candidates practising pseudocode and programming logic.
-   Anyone looking for organised placement preparation material.

## Note

These are general preparation resources. Recruitment tests, question
patterns, and difficulty levels may vary across companies and hiring
drives. Use the materials as part of your preparation, along with timed
practice and revision.
